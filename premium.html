I'm a Class +2 Bio Science student living in the scenic hills of Wayanad, Kerala. While my formal education covers Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, English, and Hindi, my real classroom is the terminal.

I discovered programming through Termux on my phone—a constraint that taught me to optimize everything. With limited bandwidth and resources, I learned to build efficient bots, automate repetitive tasks, and think creatively about solutions.

From Pyrogram-based Telegram bots to Docker containerized microservices, I've taught myself to build systems that work reliably. I also love tweaking Custom ROMs and pushing Android devices beyond their limits.

My philosophy is simple: "If it can be automated, it should be. If it can be optimized, it must be."
